Definitions

  • Basic mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • EV mNAV = (Market cap + Debt + Preferred − Cash) ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • CEBE mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × (1 − Senior Claims %) × BTC price), where Senior Claims % = (Debt + Preferred − Cash − issuer-held STRC) ÷ BTC price ÷ BTC held, clamped to 0–100%. Definitions per CEBE Tracker’s mNAV modeler and API formula.
  • Residual NAV / Share = residual BTC NAV ÷ basic shares outstanding, where residual BTC NAV = BTC NAV × (1 − Senior Claims %), i.e. BTC held × BTC price minus (Debt + Preferred − Cash − issuer-held STRC), using the same clamped senior-claims fraction as CEBE mNAV (so stock price ÷ Residual NAV / Share = CEBE mNAV).
  • Market cap = live stock price (USD) × basic shares outstanding. Non-USD prices converted at live FX. A company may show a different live quote in the Stock price cell, labeled with that ticker. The muted line under it is the latest regular-session close of the quote used for valuation. Market cap, basic mNAV, EV mNAV, residual NAV / share, and CEBE mNAV still use that valuation quote. If the display quote is unavailable, the cell shows the valuation quote instead.
  • Δ Shares = basic shares outstanding minus the count at the previous reporting date (the prior distinct as-of snapshot). ▲ is how many shares were added, ▼ is how many were removed, and the same count reads Unchanged. The date under the figure is that previous count's as-of date. Metaplanet uses its Tokyo basic share count, the same count as Shares outstanding.
  • % Δ Shares = Δ Shares ÷ the previous basic share count, shown to two decimals (for example ▲ 1.41%). A non-zero move that would round to 0.00% is shown as <0.01%, with the same ▲ or ▼. Unchanged, the “vs” date, and a missing prior count match Δ Shares. Metaplanet uses the same Tokyo share counts.
  • Institutional ownership = institutional shares ÷ the basic shares outstanding in this table. For a US company the numerator is the Form 13F total. A third-party site’s percent is not used, because that percent is often divided by an older share count. The muted line is the holdings as-of date (for example 13F Jun 30). Hover, focus, or tap the figure for holders above 5% of those basic shares: name, shares, percent, and source. If none are above 5%, the three largest reported holders are listed instead. Related filers such as separate Vanguard entities are not added together. Metaplanet has no 13F total, so the cell says Not reported; the same control lists holders above 5% from its major-shareholder notices and shareholder register.
  • Latest filing — a freshness check. For an SEC filer, the latest 8-K, 8-K/A, 10-Q, 10-Q/A, 10-K, or 10-K/A on EDGAR. For Metaplanet, the newest disclosure on its TDnet list. “Up to date” means that filing is not newer than the date behind the CEBE figures (the cited filing for SEC filers; the newest TDnet date CEBE cites, otherwise the as-of date, for Metaplanet). A newer filing is flagged. The numbers on this page still come from CEBE Tracker.
