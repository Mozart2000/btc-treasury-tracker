Definitions

  • Basic mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • EV mNAV = (Market cap + Debt + Preferred − Cash) ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • CEBE mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × (1 − Senior Claims %) × BTC price), where Senior Claims % = (Debt + Preferred − Cash − issuer-held STRC) ÷ BTC price ÷ BTC held, clamped to 0–100%. Definitions per CEBE Tracker’s mNAV modeler and API formula.
  • Residual NAV / Share = residual BTC NAV ÷ basic shares outstanding, where residual BTC NAV = BTC NAV × (1 − Senior Claims %), i.e. BTC held × BTC price minus (Debt + Preferred − Cash − issuer-held STRC), using the same clamped senior-claims fraction as CEBE mNAV (so stock price ÷ Residual NAV / Share = CEBE mNAV).
  • Market cap = live stock price (USD) × basic shares outstanding. Non-USD prices converted at live FX.
  • Latest filing — a freshness check. For an SEC filer, the latest 8-K, 8-K/A, 10-Q, 10-Q/A, 10-K, or 10-K/A on EDGAR. For Metaplanet, the newest disclosure on its TDnet list. “Up to date” means that filing is not newer than the date behind the CEBE figures (the cited filing for SEC filers; the newest TDnet date CEBE cites, otherwise the as-of date, for Metaplanet). A newer filing is flagged. The numbers on this page still come from CEBE Tracker.
