Definitions

  • Basic mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • EV mNAV = (Market cap + Debt + Preferred − Cash) ÷ (BTC held × BTC price).
  • CEBE mNAV = Market cap ÷ (BTC held × (1 − Senior Claims %) × BTC price), where Senior Claims % = (Debt + Preferred − Cash − issuer-held STRC) ÷ BTC price ÷ BTC held, clamped to 0–100%. Definitions per CEBE Tracker’s mNAV modeler and API formula.
  • Market cap = live stock price (USD) × basic shares outstanding. Non-USD prices converted at live FX.
